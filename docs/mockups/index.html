How to review responsiveness

Mobile-first with fluid layouts and breakpoints. Review on physical devices or Chrome/Safari DevTools device toolbar:

iPhone SE & Mini (320×568, 375×667)
Fixed bottom nav with safe-area inset, 48px tap targets, 18px base font, wrapping chip rows, zero horizontal scroll, graceful layout at 200% text zoom.
Standard iPhone (390×844) & iPhone Plus/Pro Max
Comfortable single-column card flow, clamped 6-line Wikipedia lead with in-place expand, grouped dimension filters, bottom safe-area padding.
Phone Landscape (844×390)
Scrollable drawer avoiding button clip, compact header spacing, no viewport lock (no 100vh bugs).
iPad (≥ 640px)
Top nav appears, bottom nav hides, 3-column photo grid.
Desktop / iPad landscape (≥ 1024px)
Map docks to a sticky right column beside the list, 4-column photo grid.

Family compliance & Species Enrichment notes

Hand-written scoped CSS only (shared mockup.css stands in for component styles) — no Tailwind. White cards on #f8f9fa, 1px #e9ecef borders, 8px radius (gaylonphotos tokens).
WCAG AAA: all text ≥ 7:1 contrast incl. muted (#495057); every status and tide indicator uses color + text label (CR/EN/VU/NT/LC badges, Tide: prefix).
Touch targets strictly ≥ 48px for chips, filter tags, accordion summaries, and action buttons. Inputs ≥ 16px font-size to prevent iOS focus zooming.
Destructive actions use modal confirmation (see Delete trip on the Trips screen) — never toasts.
Dual attribution: “Data from eBird.org” at page footer; “Wikipedia · CC BY-SA 4.0” footer with revision permalink disclosure on About; separate AI source line on Finding this bird.
