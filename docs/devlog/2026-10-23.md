# 2026-10-23 Devlog

## 21:00 EDT - External sources Phase 2: the Norway connector (td-c2913c)

### Summary
The owner approved td-53b207 (Phase 1) and said to start Phase 2. The connector, `src/connectors/gbif-artsobs/`, reads Artsobservasjoner's bird records from GBIF by paging (owner's option B). It places them by their own municipality and pushes them through the Phase 1 ingest API. It runs as its own PM2 app with its own token file and never touches the database.
- **Live runs:** two live runs into `birds_test` found three facts about GBIF that the plan had wrong (findings 14 and 15, plus hybrids).
- **Review:** CODEX1 (high effort) took six rounds and found 16 problems, all fixed with a regression test each.
- **Reference links:** Admin → Sources now has the "Reference" links the owner asked for.
- **Status:** nothing is committed or deployed yet.

### What Was Done
- **Connector modules:** `config`, `gbif` (request policy), `records`, `fetch` (slices and completeness), `aggregate` (placement, localities, recent), `payload`, `ingest`, `run` and `main`.
  - `scripts/build-connector.mjs` bundles it and fails the build if anything other than the app's pure contract module is pulled in.
  - The PM2 app `birds-connector-gbif-artsobs` has a 320 MB heap and restarts at 450 MB.
  - The deploy gate requires it to stay online for 35 s.
- **Live NO-18 (Nordland) into `birds_test`:** published 148,927 records, 47 municipalities and 300 species (288 matched).
  - 25,583 records were placed by municipality, exactly Phase 0's coastal gap; 1 was unplaced.
  - It took about 5 minutes, with peak RSS 328 MB.
  - 12 unmatched names are all synonyms (e.g. Phalacrocorax aristotelis), which the owner can fix with "Match species".
- **What the live runs found:**
  - **Finding 14:** GBIF pages stall once offset + limit passes 10,000, despite the documented 100,000 cap, so slices are capped at 9,700 records.
  - **Hybrids:** GBIF files them at rank SPECIES with no `canonicalName` (Wigeon × Mallard, 767 records). They now count as unkeyed, with a note.
  - **Finding 15:** GBIF answers 429 under sustained paging and sends no usable rate-limit headers. A 429 now pauses every request, backing off from 15 s up to 5 min, with 8 tries.
- **Trøndelag (NO-16) live run** (code before the review fixes): published fine, but slowly.
  - Read 516,440 records with 3,405 requests, 1,579 of which (46%) were 429s.
  - Took 4 h 21 min (15,657 s), of which only 77 s was CPU, instead of the 30–45 min estimated.
  - Peak RSS 344 MB and heap 142 MB, under the 450 MB restart.
  - Published 399,382 records, 24 municipalities and 291 species (281 matched; 10 unmatched names, 2,894 reports). 66,642 were placed by municipality, 1 was unplaced, and there were 0 gaps.
  - GBIF throttled it after a day of test traffic from one address. The 429-bypass bug CODEX1 later found (queued requests ignoring the pause) likely added to the 429 count.
  - The owner asked why the "faster" route is this slow. The answer: the paging estimate assumed no throttling, and the download-API estimate came from GBIF's public log, not our own run.
  - Timing one all-Norway download under the owner's GBIF account is offered as the way to settle it.
- **CODEX1 review, round 1 (10 findings):**
  - **P1:** a broken facet answer could publish a county of zeros.
  - **P2:**
    - a re-read kept deleted records;
    - no dedupe across 2024 counties;
    - species reported only after the window were dropped;
    - the pin share ignored reports without coordinates;
    - queued requests bypassed a 429 pause;
    - a re-read could page past the cap;
    - a large locality overflowed a spread call;
    - a failed name lookup left requests queued;
    - the deploy gate accepted a stopped connector.
- **CODEX1 review, rounds 2–6:**
  - **Records moving between date slices mid-read.** The final rule: a slice counts only once it reads back whole. After all slices are read, each slice's count is asked for again (count only). The whole county is read again, up to 3 times, when a count changed or when a record turns up in two slices. The one exception is an interval-dated record with the same event date under the same spelling both times.
  - **The first failure in a pool** now aborts the requests beside it.
  - **The deploy gate** now reads `pm2 jlist` past `[PM2][WARN]` lines.
  - **The remaining limit,** documented in the plan: GBIF's search has no snapshot, so only changes that show in counts or keys are caught.
