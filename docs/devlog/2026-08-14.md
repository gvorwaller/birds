### Key Technical Insight

Mode A was throwing away 11 months. The year planner is one extra weighted
aggregate over data already fetched for the 8 analyzed hotspots — no new eBird
calls.

fallthrough (pin/home/Gulf of Guinea); (2) droplet clock is UTC, so
`getMonth()` / `EXTRACT(YEAR FROM NOW())` flip a day early for Eastern. Calendar
month and `lastCompleteYear` now use `America/New_York`.
