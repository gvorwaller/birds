**Drawings**
- Process: a gull pilot first, then all four birds.
- Both owner checkpoints were tappable Artifact previews:

  - all four birds: "I hate to ever call something perfect, but imo this is it."

- A shared `Wing` helper, `scripts/bird-parts/flight.py`, splits each wing into its feather rows. Each row is a band of the chord, measured from the trailing edge:
  - arm: secondaries, then the greater, median and lesser coverts, with tertials at the root;
  - hand: primaries, primary coverts and alula;
  - underside: wing linings (underwing coverts) and axillaries;
  - also: the trailing edge, the dark wingtip, and lines between individual flight feathers.
- The gull pilot was traced from CC BY-SA Commons flight photos, used for proportions only and cited in the gull's sources. The other three were drawn from known proportions.
- Each bird's flight drawings:
  - **Song Sparrow:** a rounded wing, wing bars, a pale supercilium on the crown, and a fanned tail with its rectrices.
  - **Lesser Yellowlegs:** long, pointed dark wings with no wing stripe, white uppertail coverts behind a dark rump, a barred tail, and trailing yellow legs.
  - **Mallard drake:** a blue speculum with a white border on each side (greater-covert tips in front, secondary tips behind), the curled tail feathers, a chestnut breast and orange feet.
  - **Herring Gull:** a black wingtip with mirrors and apical spots, and a white trailing edge.
- `common.build()` gained:

### Key Technical Insight
- **Overlapping parts need a deliberate paint order.** The standing drawings partition the silhouette, so no two parts overlap. In the flight drawings, rows and marks overlap on purpose (the trailing edge over the secondaries, mirrors over the primaries). Draw order then decides both what is visible and what a tap hits.
  - The bug CODEX1 found was a later, larger part covering part of an earlier one.
  - Check it with an elementFromPoint hit-test at a known point, not only by looking at the drawing.
  - The selected part is repainted on top, so a highlight is never buried under another row.
- **No new breakpoints:** cs.md allows only 640px and 1024px. A route test now forbids any other width media query in `PartsDiagram`.
- **A "Show it" button that switches views removes itself.** Once the part is drawn, the button is gone, so focus must move to the part's name in the list.
- **Size:** the page chunk is 232KB raw, about 62KB gzipped, and loads only on `/parts-of-a-bird`.
