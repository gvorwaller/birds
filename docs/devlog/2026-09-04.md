# 2026-09-04 — Worldwide frequency loading

## Country picker terminal-outcome fix (`td-90d6bb`)

- Production audit found 14 countries falsely retained in the unfinished-country
  picker. Their remaining regions had no `frequency_fetch` row because eBird had
  conclusively returned either a zero-checklist export or a deterministic HTTP 500
  after the country job exhausted or cancelled its retries.
- The picker now counts zero-checklist attempts as resolved and counts deterministic
  HTTP 500 attempts as resolved only when the parent country job is failed/cancelled
  and no country retry is active. Timeouts, rate limits, credential failures, active
  retries, and unattempted regions remain unfinished.
- Added focused tests for mixed loaded/terminal children, childless country outcomes,
  active HTTP-500 retries, and retryable timeouts. Help and About now describe the
  corrected behavior.
