## td-07eb28: Nearest reports Check jumped to the top

- Cause: the card's Window/Distance form is a GET form; SvelteKit navigates it and, without `data-sveltekit-noscroll`, scrolls to the top (to the `#nearest-reports` anchor when the URL had one, hence "almost every time"). The neighbouring "Check nearest reports" link already had noscroll.
- noscroll alone was not enough in WebKit: a new Window also reloads Recent reports above, both cards drop to placeholders, then refill at a different height (measured: controls slid -609 px, then -1,513 px, never back). `holdNearestControls` keeps the form at its tapped viewport position each frame until both feeds settle (30 s cap), and lets go on touch, wheel or key so the reader's own scroll wins.
- Verified on a throwaway dev server with `fetch` stubbed (no request left the machine; eBird feeds answered after 1.5 s): Playwright Chromium + WebKit 40/40 (drift sampled every 100 ms for 3.5 s, results shown, own scroll kept); with the hold removed WebKit fails; iOS Simulator (Mobile Safari) Last 30 days and Last 7 days with Any distance: the controls stay put during loading and after.
