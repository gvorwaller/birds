# 2026-10-26 Devlog

## Norway source: Artskart probe (td-c2913c)
The owner asked to try Artsobservasjoner's own source before any more GBIF testing. GBIF's multi-country reach doesn't matter yet: no new countries are planned (Sweden maybe), and the only trip planned is Norway in 2027. The owner also wants observer names stored: they're public on Artsobservasjoner, and seeing who birds a county most is interesting and points to people worth looking up. Probe only; no app or connector code changed.

- **Access:** Artsdatabanken's Artskart public API (`https://artskart.artsdatabanken.no/publicapi/api/`) needs no key or registration. Its Swagger spec is at `publicapi/swagger/docs/v1`. The lookup endpoints return 500 or `null`.
- **Filter for Artsobservasjoner birds in one county:**
  - Query: `observations/list?countys[]=18&taxongroups[]=8&collections[]=so2-birds&fromDate=2016-01-01`.
  - County 18 is Nordland; taxon group 8 is birds (`taxons[]=252` with child taxa gives the same count, but 18 s slower).
  - Date bounds are inclusive at both ends.
  - Results are ordered by observation date. Paging repeats exactly, and pages don't overlap.
  - A page sometimes repeats a few rows, so records are deduplicated by `Id`.
- **Full Nordland pull, timed:**
  - 723,133 records in 27 min 31 s (1,651 s): 144 requests, month windows, 10,000 rows per page, one request at a time.
  - No retries and no throttling. Every window's `TotalCount` matched the records received; 605 repeated rows were dropped.
  - The data is 410 MB as JSON lines.
  - For comparison, the 10-23 GBIF paging run for Trøndelag took 4 h 21 min for 516,440 records, with 3,405 requests, 46% of them 429s.
- **The 17% gap with GBIF is our filter, not missing data:**
  - GBIF's copy, filtered by `stateProvince=Nordland`, has 720,947 records, within 0.1% of Artskart for every year up to 2025. 2026 lags by 3.4%.
  - The `gadmGid=NOR.10_1` filter the shelved connector uses keeps only 596,595.
  - The other ~124k have no GADM county; probably points at sea or on the shore, where the GADM polygons stop (not checked).
  - On Artskart, every record carries `County`, `Municipality` and `MunicipalityId`: 43 municipalities, none null.
- **Fields:**
  - scientific name and Artsdatabanken taxon IDs;
  - date, with no time;
  - locality name, WGS84 lat/lon (decimal comma) and `Precision` in metres (≤1 km for 90%);
  - `Count` (null for 2.5%) and behaviour;
  - `DetailUrl` (the sighting on mobil.artsobservasjoner.no);
  - `Collector`;
  - `TrackDateTime`.
- **Observers:**
  - `Collector` is never null. Parties are separated by `|`; 20% of records list 2–13 people.
  - 2,199 distinct people after splitting and collapsing whitespace. 143 have at least 1,000 records and 463 have at least 100.
  - Activity is concentrated: the single most active string covers 9.9% of records, and the top 20 cover 47.6%.
  - Names are plain text with no user ID, so two people who share a name would merge.
- **Names against the eBird taxonomy (`taxonomy_cache`):**
  - 498 names in all.
  - 99.41% of records match an eBird species exactly.
  - Another 0.39% are subspecies that eBird maps to a species through `report_as`. Example: *Anas crecca carolinensis* → Green-winged Teal, as eBird itself does.
  - 0.04% roll up to their binomial.
  - 0.157% are genus or family reports (*Laridae*, *Phalacrocorax*, *Aves*): 71 names that are not a species.
- **Incremental refresh works:**
  - `afterTrackDateTime=2026-10-19` returned 1,643 changed Nordland records; the local pull counted 1,640 records since then.
  - `observations/Deleted?afterTrackDateTime=` pages the deleted IDs, Artskart-wide (8,403 in the week).
  - A refresh can fetch changes instead of the whole county.
- **Open:**
  - Artsdatabanken's terms page sits behind an Azure WAF that blocks scripted fetches, so it still needs reading in a browser.
  - The data licence is CC BY 4.0 (the GBIF dataset says so too).
- **Owner decision pending:** move the connector's fetch layer to Artskart.
