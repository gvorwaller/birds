## td-46fff6: "What can I see?" shows Artsobservasjoner reports near a place (rev B)
The owner asked for the plan and implementation in one go. Rev A (municipality centres) was replanned on the stored reports from td-769300: the card now counts **reports within the radius by their own coordinates**. Plan: `docs/2026-10-28-forecast-external-card-plan-CC.md` → "Rev B".

The 31 mi reports come from 204 named places in 12 municipalities.

**Query speed:**
- The planner's first choice walked every taxon's records, taking 500 ms on prod. Filtering a coordinate box first (a materialized CTE) took 68 ms.
- Migration 0098 swaps the (lat, lon) btree for a GiST index on `point(lon, lat)`. It was measured on birds_test with real Nordland plus 2M synthetic points spread over Norway: 14 ms against 17 ms for a 50 km box (index scan 1.6 ms against 4.5 ms). The gain is small, but it suits a 2-D search.

**Built:**
- **Reader:** `src/lib/server/external-sources/near.ts`, one read snapshot. It runs one aggregate query (box, month, published window via the records' `load_id`, active sources, haversine cut), grouped by place, locality, species and year. The rest is shaped in JS.
- **Nearest-report fallback:** 3× the radius, at least 30 km.
- **Card:** `ExternalNearCard.svelte`. Needs first; per species its places with distance and "in N of M Octobers". The rest of the needs and the seen species sit in `<details>` folds. It also shows the busiest 5 places (Map, plus Add to trip when the place's best report is placed within 1 km) and the municipalities, each linking to "Where and when to look".
- **Forecast loader:** awaits the card with the shell, so it renders without JavaScript; a failure is logged and the card left out. The eBird analysis stream adds the sources that cover eBird's region but have nothing loaded, for a Load button (not for viewers). The page gets the shared `queueExternalLoad` action.
- **Pointer line:** a line in the eBird card links to the card and is never added into the eBird numbers.
- **Help:** one bullet in "Reports from other sources".

**Checks:**
- `near-db.test.ts` has 12 tests. Mutations on the radius cut, the window, seen-per-user and the active filter are each caught. The radius mutation first survived, because a due-north record was already cut by the box; a record in the box's corner, outside the circle, now guards it.
- **Render tests:** 7, covering the card states, wording and list keys. **Loader tests:** 7 (failure isolation, the hint, the pointer).
- **Live check:** real Nordland was loaded into birds_test (own source `xnear-…`). Sandnessjøen in October at 31 mi gives 1,429 / 109 species / 70 needs (fixture user with 39 seen). That equals an independent full-table SQL count and the prod measurement.
- **Playwright:** Chromium + WebKit × 5 themes × 320/390/1200 found nothing (minimum contrast 8.35, minimum tap 48 px). The JavaScript-off run renders the rows and the folds open. A US place shows no card.
- **iOS Simulator (Mobile Safari):** both folds open and close on tap, and "Where in Nordland" navigates. The pass caught a missing fold marker (`display: flex` hides it), now ▸/▾ as on "Where and when to look".
- `npm run check`: 0/0. **Full suite:** 3,306 passed; only the known searchable-select failure.

**CODEX1 (high), round 1: three P2s, all fixed with regressions, each mutation-checked.**
- **F1, duplicate list keys.** Two county-level places with the same name in different counties gave the same Svelte key, which throws on the client. Places now carry a key built from the county or municipality key and the locality, and the card keys by it.
- **F2, box too small.** `boxAround` used dLat/cos(lat), which falls short of the circle's widest longitude; a report at 49.9995 km fell outside a 50 km box. It now uses the exact spherical extent, with full longitude at a pole or across ±180°. Tested against points on the circle every 0.25° in 8 cases.
- **F3, global fallback.** The nearest fallback ran only when every source was empty, so one source's reports hid another's nearest. It now runs per missing active source; a second active source in the fixtures covers it.

**Round 2:** clean. CODEX1 notes that client hydration is not proven by the server-render tests. The browser passes ran with JavaScript on and had no page errors, but the live data had no same-name twins, so the twin case is covered at the reader and the source level only.

CODEX1 ran `td usage --new-session` at startup in both rounds, before it read the no-td rule, and made no other td calls or writes.

**Cleanup:** the live source `xnear-…`, its loads, records, taxa and connector, the fixture user and its seen rows, the connector env file and the dev server are all removed. Migration 0098 stays applied in birds_test, as it is in the repo.

## td-2219df: Admin → Sources, a save shows it is working, its result shows where it was made, every section folds
**Owner report:** "Match to Rock Pigeon" did nothing visible, so it was tapped 6 times.

**What the logs showed (prod, read-only):**
- nginx: six `POST /admin?/set_taxon_override`, each 200 after 2.6–4.9 s; all six saved (bydue → Rock Pigeon, 97 reports).
- No `GET /admin/__data.json` followed any of them, so the phone never refreshed the list. No client stall or error was recorded.
- Not reproduced: the iOS Simulator (Safari and the home-screen app, dev and production builds, a 4 s save held with a database lock) refreshed every time. The cause on the phone is unknown.

**Built:**
- **Busy state:** the tapped button reads "Matching…", "Removing…", "Queueing…" or "Re-matching…", with "Working on it; this can take a few seconds." below it; every button in the panel waits until it finishes.
- **Result where the action was made:** the message shows at the top of that section (Unmatched taxa, Saved matches, Change a match, Load a region, Re-match species) and is scrolled into the space between the top bar and the tab bar when it is off screen. Without JavaScript it stays at the top of the tab.
- **Refresh fallback:** if a save succeeds but the page data is not replaced (the data request fails, or nothing within 10 s), Admin → Sources reloads at that source.
- **Every section folds:** Reference, Connector (summary shows when it was last seen), Load a region and Loads (open by default), Unmatched taxa, Saved matches and Change a match (also when empty), Re-match species. Each source is now a bordered card, as on the Tags tab.

**Checks:**
- **Render tests:** `sources-render.test.ts` (4): fold order and headings, which start open, empty match sections stay folds, the result at the top without JavaScript. Removing `open` from Loads fails it.
- **Browser (WebKit iPhone + Chromium touch, fixture source, 3 s save):** busy label, all buttons disabled, result in the section and on screen; the data request never answering, or failing, reloads the tab at that source (measured 4.8–14.9 s after the tap, which includes the 3 s save).
- **Playwright pass:** Chromium + WebKit × 5 themes × 320/390/1200, 30 runs flagged 0 (minimum contrast 7.27, minimum tap 48 px). JavaScript off: the rows render.
- **iOS Simulator (Mobile Safari):** "Matching…" and "Removing…" show during a held save; the result appears in its section. The first version's scroll put the message under the top bar (it measured a `header` the app does not have); it now measures `nav.top-nav` and `nav.bottom-nav`.

**CODEX1, round 1: two findings, both fixed.**
- **P2, revoke result in the wrong place.** After a match, revoking a token showed its result in the old match section (maybe another source, maybe a closed fold). Revoke now carries its source, uses the shared busy state, and shows its result in that source's Connector fold.
- **P3, an earlier result beside a running action.** Starting a second action showed the first one's result in the new section while it ran. Results now show only when nothing is running. A result whose fold was closed while it ran opens that fold.

**Round 2: one finding, fixed.** Revoke waited for the page data without a limit and swallowed a failure, so a stalled refresh could leave "Revoking…" and every button disabled. Every action, revoke included, now goes through one `settle()`: at most 10 s, then reload the tab when the data was not refreshed.

**Round 3:** clean.

**Re-checked after the fixes (WebKit iPhone + Chromium touch):**
- **During a second match:** 0 messages shown.
- **Revoke:**
  - its message is in its source's Connector fold, open and on screen;
  - with the page data never answering, the page reloads about 10 s later;
  - with the page data failing, the reload starts 40–68 ms after the tap.
- **Earlier checks:** the theme × width pass (30 runs flagged 0) and the JavaScript-off run were also re-run after the round-1 fixes.

**Gates:** `npm run check` 0/0. Full suite after the last fix: 3,315 passed; only the known searchable-select failure.
