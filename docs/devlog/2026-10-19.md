# 2026-10-19 Devlog

## 13:40 EDT - A raptor in Parts of a Bird (td-dd4f9c)

### Summary
Parts of a Bird has a fifth drawing: an adult light-morph Red-tailed Hawk, perched and soaring (from above and from below). It adds the parts hawk identification relies on, and species pages for hawks, eagles, falcons, the Osprey, vultures and owls now link their part names to it. Not yet committed: waiting for the owner's go.

### What Was Done

**Drawings** (`scripts/bird-parts/raptor.py`, `raptor_flight.py`)
- Traced from two openly licensed Commons photos, used for proportions only:
  - perched: "Red-tailed-hawk-perched.jpg", Kate Perez, CC BY-SA 4.0;
  - soaring: "Buteo jamaicensis 7.jpg", CC BY 2.0.
- The perched hawk is upright, so its plate is portrait (580×740). `PartsDiagram` caps a portrait whole drawing at 560 px tall. "One wing, larger" is not capped.
- Flight views use `HawkWing`, a `flight.Wing` whose hand ends in five fanned fingers. `fingers` is its own part, drawn over the primaries.
- Regenerating every bird leaves the other four drawings' JSON byte-identical.

**Parts and text** (`src/lib/bird-parts/raptor.ts`)
- New parts:
  - head and legs: cere, tarsus, talons;
  - underparts: belly band, leg feathers;
  - wing: patagial bar, carpal patch, fingers, dark trailing edge, wingtip vs. tail;
  - tail: subterminal band.
- Shared parts keep their ids, so a selection carries across views and birds' links.
- Facts checked against Wikipedia, the USGS Patuxent ID page and HawkWatch International. Two claims were dropped because no source in hand confirmed them: Swainson's Hawk's perched wingtips, and the harrier's rump.

**Species-page links** (`src/lib/bird-parts/terms.ts`)
- The raptor drawing serves Accipitridae, Falconidae, Pandionidae, Cathartidae, Sagittariidae, Strigidae and Tytonidae.
- New terms: cere, patagial bar/mark/band/patagium, belly band, tarsus/tarsi.
- Linked only on raptor and owl pages. A count over the `birds_test` text found other meanings elsewhere:
  - "carpal patch/bar/comma" (a tern's upperwing carpal bar);
  - "fingers"/emarginations ("finger claws");
  - "subterminal band" (gull bill bands).

**Found and fixed along the way:** every bird's caption and glossary read "Tarsus(lower leg)". Svelte 5 strips the leading space inside the `aka` span; now `{" "}` comes before it, with a test that fails on the old markup.

**Review** (CODEX1, high effort)
- Two P2s and one P3, all fixed:
  - Main parts' "Wing coverts" on the perched hawk also covered the exposed secondaries. It is now built from the covert rows only, and the secondaries are a main part.
  - The tail and subterminal-band text overstated one pattern. It now notes western birds' several bands and Harlan's pale tails.
  - The patagial bar can be faint on pale birds, not only absent on dark ones.
- Re-review clean.

**Verification**
- `npm run check` 0/0, build OK, `git diff --check` clean.
- Full suite: 2,897 passed, plus the 1 known `searchable-select` failure.
- Playwright, Chromium and WebKit, 5 themes at 320, 390 and 1200 px (30 runs):
  - a cold `#raptor-patagial-bar` opens Flight, below with the bar selected;
  - tapping the cere on the drawing selects it;
  - the feather layer adds the covert rows;
  - no button under 48 px, no overflow, minimum contrast 7.79, no console errors.
  - Fixture users were deleted.
- iOS Simulator (axe): taps on the cere, the tarsus, and the patagial bar and carpal patch in One wing, larger all select the right part.

### Key Technical Insight
- **Svelte 5 strips whitespace at the start of an element.** `<span> (x)</span>` renders "(x)" flush against the previous word. Put the space outside the element, as `{" "}`.

### What Was Done

**Drawings** (`scripts/bird-parts/cormorant.py`, `cormorant_flight.py`)
- Traced from two openly licensed Commons photos, used for proportions only:

**Text** (`src/lib/bird-parts/cormorant.ts`)
