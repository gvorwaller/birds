## Keep every report (td-769300)
Owner: "for sure, keep the records … I'm a data-hungry guy." Plan: `docs/2026-10-28-external-records-plan-CC.md`.
- **Measured first:**
  - **Artskart sample:** 3,000 Nordland records. Name 99.7%, Red List 98%, Count 94%, Notes 79% (structured Activity and Validationstatus), Behaviour 68%, Sex 10%, photos 9%, survey project 0.7%. IdentifiedBy, Habitat and Info were never filled.
  - **Artskart `TotalCount`:** 3,394,210 bird reports across the 19 counties over 24 months. Largest is Rogaland at 425,924; Nordland has 148,536.
- **Built:**
  - Migration 0097 adds two kinds of rows a load can send, `records` and `taxa`.
  - **`external_records`:** one row per report, every detail, swapped per county like the other tables.
  - **`external_taxa`:** source-wide, one row per taxon, with its Norwegian name, Red List status and eBird species code (kept current by `deriveSpecies`, so an override reaches every old report).
  - **Places:** they gain Artskart's municipality number. The connector now fills `lat`/`lon` with the median of each municipality's reports.
  - **Connector memory:** reports are spooled to a temporary file, not held in memory. A county re-read empties the file, and every load removes it.
- **Live load into `birds_test`** (real Nordland, own source `xrec-31513`):
  - **Reports:** 153,066 stored (148,536 in the window plus 4,530 after it), at about 603 bytes each to send.
  - **Time:** reading Artskart took 633 s. Sending 486 chunks took 120 s; finishing took 6 s.
  - **Size:** 88 MB with indexes (61 MB table, 27 MB indexes).
  - **Cross-checks:** all 12 monthly county totals and all 298 species agree with counts taken straight from the stored reports. All 41 municipalities got a location and a number; for example Alstahaug 65.927, 12.457.
  - **Memory:** the connector peaked at 485 MB on the Mac, against about 330 MB before. The cause was the "Latest reports" candidates now holding full records. They now keep only what that list shows.
  - **Load size cap:** raised from 300 MB to 1 GB, because Rogaland needs about 265 MB.
- **Fixture hygiene:** two of my connector test runs crashed during cleanup before the cleanup knew about the new table. They left sources `xc-lrurj2tm` and `xc-lvprj7a1`, 56 records each, and two users. All of it was deleted by exact id.
- **Second live load,** after trimming the "Latest reports" candidates: heap at the end of the read 173 MB (was 216 MB), peak memory 462 MB on the Mac. Sending took 143 s and finishing 6 s.
- **CODEX1 review (high):** round 1 found no P1 and three P2s, all accepted.
  1. **Spool file left open on a failed send:** chunks() now closes readline and destroys its input in `finally`. On real files, Node 22.16 and 24.3 already release the descriptor when the loop exits early, with or without that cleanup. CODEX1 had reproduced the leak on a memory-backed fs and withdrew it as a production issue. The new test guards the behaviour; it does not fail if the explicit close is removed.
  2. **Photo credits ignored the observer-name rule:** they now follow it. A credit is refused at the chunk unless the source stores observer names, and again at finish if that flag was turned off in between.
  3. **Taxa held only in records:** a species reported only after the window and not in the last 14 days never appeared in the unmatched list and couldn't be overridden. Fixed: it is matched, listed with its record count (window totals first, then records, then recent reports, never added together), and accepted by the override.
  - **Mutation checks:** the photo check at the chunk, the photo check at finish, the records branch of the unmatched list, the override lookup and the double-count guard each fail a test when removed. The last two first survived; their test now uses a matched records-only taxon, and a records-only report that is also in recent reports.
  - Round 2 was clean.
- **Gates:** `npm run check` 0/0. The full suite passed 3,280 tests; the only failure is the known searchable-select one.
- **Cleanup:** fixture source `xrec-31513` and all its rows, connector #340 and its env file are removed; the dev server is stopped. A final check found no records, taxa, staged records or test sources left in `birds_test`, and no spool folders.
