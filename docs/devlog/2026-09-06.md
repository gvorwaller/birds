## Migration ribbon refinements: dash cells, continent multi-select, upper landmarks, remove player (`td-1a47a7`)

- **Neutral Dash Cells for Low-Sample & Excluded Data:**
  - Replaced misleading green-hatched cells with an uncolored card background (`var(--card)`) displaying a crisp centered dash (`–`), eliminating the visual illusion of positive sightings in low-sample cells.
  - Removed SVG hatch patterns and modernized the "no data" legend swatch to a matching forward diagonal slash (`/`).
  - **Weighting-Aware Semantics & Copy:**
    - Under **Equal Weighting**: explains that one or more countries below 40 checklists were excluded from the average (and if every country is below 40, the cell is too thin to rate).
    - Under **By Checklists Weighting**: explains that the aggregate cell itself has under 40 checklists (small sample), while its reporting rate remains available in the cell readout.
    - Updated copy across `MigrationRibbon.svelte`, `src/routes/about/+page.svelte`, and `src/routes/help/+page.svelte`.
- **Continent Multi-Select Dropdown with Checkboxes:**
  - Replaced the single-select `<select>` with an accessible popover containing checkboxes for all 7 geographic continents.
  - Added quick-action shortcuts (`Select all` and `Primary only`) with guardrails ensuring at least one continent remains selected.
  - Implemented the full nonmodal dialog keyboard contract: focuses the first interactive control on open, and restores focus to `#rbcontBtn` upon close or `Escape`.
  - Paging keyboard shortcuts (`PageUp` / `PageDown`) strictly cycle within visible drawn columns.
- **Upper Latitude Landmarks & Strict Fallback Invariant:**
  - Added anchor cities and regions to `LANDMARKS.WORLD` for 40°N through 80°N (e.g., London, Vancouver, Berlin at 50–60°N; Anchorage, Oslo, Helsinki at 60–70°N; Arctic Tundra at 70–80°N).
  - Enforced strict continent landmark resolution in `MigrationRibbon.svelte`: single-continent view queries `landmarkFor(band, gutterCol)` without silent fallback to `WORLD` landmarks, preventing unrelated American or Asian cities from appearing in African or European gutters.
- **Multi-Column Summary Invariant & North America Overview:**
  - Strictly gated `migrationSummary` Overview mode on `drawnColumns(s).length > 1`.
  - Removed synthetic max-of-halves metrics. When North America is selected, the summary displays `North America Overview`, explains the West/East split at 100°W, and directs the user to inspect individual cells in the readout.
- **Year Player Removal & Dead State Cleanup:**
  - Completely removed the auto-playing year player UI, interval loop, Space key handler, and dead state (`playing` and `PLAY_MS`) from `RibbonState`, reducers, and unit tests.
  - Preserved manual month scrubbing via slider and `◀` / `▶` step buttons for intentional inspection.
  - Cleaned up stale comments across the codebase.
