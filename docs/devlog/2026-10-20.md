**Term links** (`terms.ts`)

## 14:40 EDT - A tubenose in Parts of a Bird (td-315613, epic td-56a506)

### Summary
td-5076e6 (the auk) shipped at da0d15f, and the owner approved it. The owner asked for the tubenose next. Built and reviewed; not yet committed: waiting for the owner's go.

### What Was Done

**Model:** a Sooty Shearwater. It is on a production life list (with Pink-footed Shearwater, Northern Fulmar, Black-footed Albatross and two storm-petrels), lives in both the Pacific and the Atlantic, and its silvery underwing stripe is the classic shearwater underwing mark.

**Drawings** (`scripts/bird-parts/tubenose.py`, `tubenose_flight.py`)
- Traced from openly licensed Commons photos, used for proportions only:
  - swimming: "Sooty shearwater kaikoura.jpg", Sabine's Sunbird, CC BY-SA 3.0. It is tilted, so the tracing is rotated about 11° to level the waterline, then scaled and mirrored; parts are clipped at the waterline;
  - bill close-up: "Puffinus griseus -near Avila Beach, California, USA -upper body-8a.jpg", marlin harms, CC BY 2.0;
  - flight: "Puffinus griseus in flight - SE Tasmania (cropped).jpg", JJ Harrison, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- A "Bill, close up" inset draws the bill, nostril tubes and hooked tip a second time, larger. Each is one part, so a tap on the bird or in the inset selects it.
- From below, the silvery underwing coverts carry a dark leading-edge mark (never a tap target).
- Regenerating every bird leaves the other eight drawings' JSON byte-identical.

**Text** (`src/lib/bird-parts/tubenose.ts`)
- New parts: nostril tubes (naricorns) and the hooked tip (maxillary unguis). A tubenose's bill is made of seven to nine horny plates.
- Compares Short-tailed, Pink-footed, Great, Manx and Balearic Shearwaters, the Northern Fulmar, the Black-footed Albatross, and Wilson's and Leach's Storm-Petrels (bill, head pattern, underwing, rump, tail).
- Facts from Wikipedia (Sooty shearwater, Procellariiformes, Albatross, Northern fulmar) and the Audubon Field Guide.

**Term links** (`terms.ts`)
- Diomedeidae, Procellariidae, Hydrobatidae and Oceanitidae use the tubenose drawing. Penguins keep the default.
- New term: naricorns, nasal tubes, tubular nostrils and tube-nose(d), only on tubenose pages. A count over the snapshot's 380 tubenose text rows found them; "nasal" alone is a call elsewhere, and the group noun "tubenoses" stays unlinked.

**Review** (CODEX1, high effort)
- One P2, fixed: the flanks also covered a strip of the forehead and a sliver under the bill, so selecting Flanks outlined part of the head. The head boundaries now overlap, and the script stops if any head, body or wing part comes out in more than one piece.
- Re-review clean.

**Verification**
- `npm run check` 0/0, build OK, `git diff --check` clean.
- Full suite: 2,937 passed, plus the 1 known `searchable-select` failure. The bird-parts tests (109) were re-run after the review fix.
- Playwright, Chromium and WebKit, 5 themes at 320, 390 and 1200 px (30 runs, re-run on the final geometry):
