- **Finding 5 (owner decision):** GBIF's GADM matching leaves out 13% of Norway's records and 17% of Nordland's, mostly coastal (eider 62%, Black Guillemot 61%).
  - The owner chose **option B**: the connector pages through the raw records and places each one by Artsobservasjoner's own municipality, mapped to the app's old counties.
  - Cost: Nordland ~655 pages (~1 GB, ~10–15 min); Trøndelag ~1,700 pages (~2.6 GB, ~30–45 min).

- **Finding 13 (download or paging):** the owner offered a GBIF account, so downloads were checked against GBIF's field lists and this week's public download log.
  - The simple CSV/Parquet formats have no municipality or GADM fields. Only the full archive (DwC-A) does.
  - The archive takes ~45–115 min per county, against 10–45 min for paging, though it moves ~10× less data.
  - **Owner kept paging.** The GBIF login is saved in `.local/gbif.env` (gitignored, mode 600) and isn't used.
  - My earlier answer said the simple download had these fields and was faster. Both claims were wrong; the plan now has the measured facts.
