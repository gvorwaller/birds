## 21:00 EDT - External sources Phase 1: generic ingest foundation (td-53b207)

### Summary
Phase 1 is built and verified locally. The app has a generic, connector-fed ingest API for non-eBird sightings sources, with Admin → Sources and job-chip progress. CODEX1 reviewed it at high effort in three rounds; round 3 was CLEAN. Nothing is committed or deployed.

Earlier today the owner gave a GBIF account; it is saved in `.local/gbif.env` (gitignored, mode 600). Measurements showed the download is slower per county than paging, and the simple format lacks the municipality fields, so the owner kept paging. This is finding 13 in the plan.

### What Was Done
- **Migration 0092** (`backend/db/migrations/0092_external_sources.sql`):
  - registry, connector tokens (hash only), region map, loads (lease and fencing, one active load per region, one live lease per claim key), chunks;
  - per-kind staging;
  - 7 published tables, swapped per (source, region);
  - raw upstream taxa, unmatched taxa, overrides, connector notes;
  - seeds `artsobs-gbif` and the 19-county map.
  - Species codes are deliberately not foreign keys to `taxonomy_cache`, because the eBird taxonomy sync deletes and reinserts that table.
- **Server, `src/lib/server/external-sources/`:**
  - `contract.ts`: contract v1 validation, canonical-JSON digests, the manifest window rule.
  - `auth.ts`: bearer token checked by its hash.
  - `http.ts`: auth before reading the body, a 256 KB cap, error mapping, structured logs without tokens, a 429 per connector.
  - `lifecycle.ts`: claim, heartbeat, chunks, finish (verify, check totals, match, atomic swap, keep notes) and fail; lease expiry and requeue; the 7-day staging sweeper, which runs in the worker's prune.
  - `taxa.ts`: matching by override, then scientific name, keyed by (upstream ID, name); ambiguous names are never guessed. Rebuilds from raw taxa.
  - `admin.ts` and `jobs.ts`.
- **Routes:** `/api/ingest/loads/claim` and `loads/[id]/{heartbeat,chunks,finish,fail}`. `/api/ingest/` is a public prefix in `hooks.server.ts`.
- **Admin → Sources tab** (`SourcesPanel.svelte`): connector health and a revoke dialog, "Queue load", the loads with their results and notes, unmatched taxa with "Match species", saved matches, "Re-match species".
- **Job chip:** `/api/jobs` lists each user's external loads separately; the poller and the layout chip show them.
- **`scripts/connector-token.mjs`:** creates or rotates a connector token and writes the env file atomically (mode 600). It never prints the token, and rotations run one at a time.
- **CODEX1 review, high effort:**
  - Round 1: 10 P2 and 2 P3. Covered claim-key races, a revoke during finish, rematch against finish, cross-region names, the window rule, int4 sums, year 0, the job-list cap, the queue race, rotation order, the cap on connector notes and dialog focus.
  - Round 2: 2 P2 and 1 P3. Covered window ends in the claim, concurrent rotations and fixture ageing.
  - Round 3: CLEAN.
  - Each fix has a regression test; the list is in the plan, "Phase 1 as built".
