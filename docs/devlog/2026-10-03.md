- **Confirm whole families (4614ebb, 7d0fb3a, 64dc82b; migrations 0075–0077).**

  of the same item serialize on the item row. The gate report counts answers by
  how they were given.

  hidden. CODEX1 rejected rebuilding the older, masked sets' pages unmasked,
